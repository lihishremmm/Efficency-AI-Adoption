In the Team's Own Words (Q12, Q33–35, 37)

Open-text questions — themes and how many people responded, kept anonymous.

Q33. Tell us about a time AI really helped you (37 responses)

Common thread: fast HTML/clickable prototypes under deadline pressure, and synthesizing research across many sources (Confluence, Jira, Dovetail, competitive data) at a speed that wasn't possible before.

Q34. Tell us about a time AI caused you to waste time (37 responses, 51% mention "time")

Common thread: hallucinated details, plugin/connector issues, and — most notably — accepting AI output without validating it, which surfaced a missed user pain point in one case.

Q35. What can you now produce that you couldn't before AI? (37 responses, 59% mention "prototypes")

Common thread: high-fidelity clickable prototypes (down to microinteractions) for validating user journeys, and cross-source research synthesis at a scale that was previously impractical.

Q12. If AI adds time in certain areas, why? (25 responses)

Common thread: verifying AI output (fact-checking personas, competitor analysis, user flows before shipping them) and increased context-switching between tasks while AI works in the background.

Q37. Any other thoughts on AI adoption within the team? (21 responses, 62% mention "AI")

Common thread: excitement about adoption paired with a call for clearer guidelines and process; a few respondents specifically noted the team should measure output quality and downstream impact, not just time saved.
