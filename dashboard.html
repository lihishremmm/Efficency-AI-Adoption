Quantity vs. actual effort — why both numbers matter

Counting tasks alone is misleading. The team is taking on ~34% more tasks — but each task now costs ~47% less effort (1.88× faster). Multiply those together (1.34 × 0.53) and total effort spent today is only ~71% of pre-AI levels — a 29% real reduction in workload, even while throughput went up.

That leaves an estimated ~40% more headroom before the team's total effort returns to where it was before AI — capacity that can be deliberately pointed at the Validate/usability-testing gap (see below) rather than absorbed passively.
