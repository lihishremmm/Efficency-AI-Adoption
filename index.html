08 AI Enablement Program
🗓 5-Phase Roadmap
Phase 1 · Month 1
Understand
7 Champion interviews (60 min each) · Document 5–7 proven workflows · Audit tool access across team · Map design system documentation gaps
Phase 2 · Months 1–2
Learn
2 hands-on workshops (Prototyping + Research Synthesis) · Publish Starter Prompt Library (20+ prompts) · Publish AI Tool Policy (one-pager) · Monthly Champion demo series
Phase 3 · Months 2–3
Experiment
2 protected hours/week per designer for AI experimentation · AI Office Hours (Champions available 1 hr/week) · Pilot output evaluation checklist with 5 volunteers
Phase 4 · Months 3–4
Standardize
Publish 3–5 AI Workflow Playbooks · Launch shared Claude Agent Library (10+ agents) · Define AI Output Quality Standards with validation checklists · AI Onboarding Guide for new hires
Phase 5 · Months 4–6
Scale
Formalize AI Champions Network (7 leads, 10% time) · Assign AI Ops owner · Quarterly AI review · Cross-functional UX Involvement Policy with PM and Engineering
